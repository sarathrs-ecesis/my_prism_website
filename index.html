GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers,
before you start.

We're still building. Here's what you can expect.

What is MyPrism 360?+

MyPrism 360 is being built to help you organise your insurance policies, understand their details and keep track of renewal dates—all in one place.

Is MyPrism 360 an insurance broker?+

No. MyPrism 360 doesn't sell insurance or recommend switching policies. Its focus is helping you understand the insurance you already have.

Do I need to add every policy at once?+

No. Start with one policy you want to understand better. You can bring the others together as you go.

Who is it being built for?+

MyPrism 360 is being built for people in Switzerland who want a clearer view of their insurance. Launch details will be shared when the product is ready.

When can I use it?+

The product is still in development. Join the waitlist on the current MyPrism 360 website to receive an email when it launches.

Are the previews on this page live?+

The previews show illustrative policies, dates and conversations to explain the proposed experience. They do not process documents or provide a live assessment of your insurance.
